# About
These are exercises for the APL Bootcamp. Find [the slides](https://github.com/rikedyp/APLBootcamp/tree/trunk/slides) in the GitHub repository.

Parts 1 to 6 are used throughout the course. There is a selection of curated [APL Quest](https://apl.quest/) problems to practise skills most useful to beginners in APL, although they are significantly more difficult than most of the problems given throughout the course.
